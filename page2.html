Attractions & Activities

Go See Altos Chavon Mediterranean Village


  • The village is a cultural center for residents, tourists, and working artists worldwide.
  • The Art Gallery features works from noted Dominican artists, as well as artists from around the world, which helps to foster a dialogue of creativity and cultural exchange.
  • Every detail of Altos de Chavón was handcrafted by local artisans.

        Go See Los Tres Ojos National Park

      • This park offers a solid glimpse of the Taino’s underground caverns once used for rituals.
      • A winding entrance staircase leads to a series of caves, with pathways hugging three freshwater, iridescent blue lagoons or “eyes” as the Taino used to call them for their oval-shapes.
      • This park has a small wooden barge revealing a gorgeous, open-air scenery of water and a rocky landscape covered in lush vegetation all around.



      Learn more here!

      Take A Boat Trip To Saona Island

    • This protected nature reserve is off the southeastern coast, among the most popular in the country.
    • Standard group tours feature a mix of a fast speedboat (taking 25 to 40 minutes) and a slower, festive sailing catamaran (taking 1 to 1.5 hours) with music and dancing.
    • Full-day excursions last between 8 and 11 hours, including travel time.
      • Learn more here!

      Reserve A Resort At Tortuga Bay Hotel

    • One of the most special places to stay in the entire Dominican Republic.
    • It is is entirely designed by Oscar de la Renta, with only 23 suites and villas
    • The interiors are stunning: tropical gardens, hand-crafted details, open terraces and a stretch of private beach.
